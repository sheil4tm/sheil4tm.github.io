This one doesn’t belong neatly anywhere — which is exactly why Misc exists.

Rain on the glass. A playlist on low. A tab full of half-finished thoughts. Nothing urgent, nothing publish-or-perish. Just a timestamp that says: this afternoon happened, and it was soft enough to keep.

## Permission slip

You’re allowed to write unfinished things. You’re allowed to publish a mood. The library isn’t a museum of conclusions — it’s a place to put the day down gently.

At the moment, that’s enough.
