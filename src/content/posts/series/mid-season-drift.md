The middle of a season is where trust is earned.

Early episodes introduce the world. Finales collect debts. Mid-season episodes have a quieter job: prove that spending another week here is worth it.

## Signs of a good drift

- Side characters get actual desires, not just quips
- The mystery expands without abandoning what already mattered
- Tone stays coherent even when plots branch

I’m currently mid-drift on a show that keeps choosing conversation over spectacle. It’s not viral-clip friendly. It’s living-room friendly. Sometimes that’s enough.
