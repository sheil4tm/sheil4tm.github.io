## A quick resume?

Well, this is a draft. So far so good, I am at chapter seven. I would like to point out the things that I don't like. I really don't like the protagonist, as a two.. that's a problem haha I have problems, I dislke her more than him, for now but I am starting to see some things on him that make me think that I won't like him either. He doesn't have a better future that our protagonist girl you know? haha
Like I write in a early spanish draft, The protagonist, June, is supposed to be 32 years old but she acts like a 22 years old. She makes the things difficult, she even say that in a part of the book and semi-quote 'I know I am doing things diffcult to Adam but..' And is weird because, like Adam says, they will be millonaire at the end of the 22 days they have to spend together living in the house, so why don't stand that? Argh, there are some many elements.. I will resume the plot
June is an actress, she is not living in NY, she finished her last serie and now she need a new job, her manager insist that she had to go back to theater, but she doesn't want to go to ny (i don't know why she have to do theather just there..in ny haha) and you know, we as readers start to see that she has problems with NY and she is scaping from something. So, she receive a sudden email, she has to go back to NY because she lived in a particular lofy? 5 years ago and she spent 6 years there, I guess, and well they need to talk to her IN PERSON haha about the place.. that's when Adam shows up. He is a chef, I really like the jobs they have, Imy problem is just with their personalities. He lives in NY and works there too. He was call for the same person because they both lived there 6 years ago. So until now, we are good. The plot is interesting but, and this is a thing that bothers me, they have no particular obligation to spent 1 month living in the same place. She needs to stay there because, her agent is giving her auditions, and she has no money so she really doesn't have where to live, but him, he just want to communte better at work? like haha and, this exchange between them, the autor make it seems like, flirting? when I don't feel like it, because in that point of time, the present, we don't know why they are not together anymore and we asume they were together but the protagonist doesn't say it explicit. We just know things happen, definitely happen between them but we don't have any more information. I find it difficult to connect with the book being like that. Its not that i dont like to go back forward to the past and the present, I read other books that manage that type of resource pretty well, but I feel it isn't working, at least in the first 7 chapters, so far. I don't think they have chemestry.
There is other funny part where June is lying in the sofa, and then she starts saying, 'wow, is difficult to read a book in the same sofa where his arm was around my neck' and somethin something, and is like.. all your memories with him are just sexual? She doesn't yearn for a sweat moment or idk, him going throught difficult times with her, together, giving support. It's like, all ended up being.. objectify. I understand (maybe) but I think at least for now the author is abusing of this resource. And June is reduce to thinking about him as an object haha saying how good he looks now, and doesn't add anything about, idk, things that will reach your heart..
We still don't know what happen between them and that will be an important flip in the story but i am not so sure that will be enough to forgive this start. Maybe, en the following chapters the thing gets better but I think what it realy puts me off is the thing that I don't feel the chemestry.
