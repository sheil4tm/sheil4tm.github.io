## A quick resume?

Ok, I won't start talking about the author or the first book I read of her. I want to keep this real short (I am trying, I swear..). So basically, the book is about Ali, our protagonist. Her marriage was a failure, like, she was going through a divorce, she have two kids and her mother passed away one year ago that time. She was going through a lot honestly. So, she is in her town, the town where she and her mother were when she was younger. She was there because so the mother could help her taking care of the children. I want to make clear she was depressed.. This is the beginning, ok? Then, our male protagonist make his entrance. Ethan who is the little brother of her best friend. He doesn't live in the town, he is there just for the summer.. you know? So, they reconnect, at first she didn't reconigze him (which was funny), they went on a date and then she saw him again in the family dinner of her best friend. Then he admits he had a crush on her when they were little. From that point we see how she rise from the ashes? And, well, he is a lawyer so he helps a little with the divorce, and all kind of things happen in between. Happen to her, because we see how she found a purpose again and how little by little she began to get her life back on track.

I am omitting a lot of details but we have the big picture. Sort of.. The thing is, they both have a 'summer romance' because he lives in another town and when he made it clear that as soon as he managed to sell the inn of his parents, he really didn't want to have any ties to the town where he had lived as a young man (plus he had a whole life in the other part of the city) so the intention for him was always to leave and she knew that and we knew that too. And I feel like I missed the oportunity to make the joke..

A Summer Romance!

Ok, moving on. I want to close the title idea and then we could move on other things that I liked it about the book and things that I didn't like it.
Like the title says, the thing about the dog, is something the author emphasizes at the end of the book. She tell us about how she didn't get it why her friends will go over and over the grief of losing a dog and then having one again.. I think this message is the most important thing that we should take away from this book. She didn't get it but then she did realized that it was worth it. Living the joy, the moments you share with your pet, despite the inevitable end. And I think the same, There was a time in my life when I thought the same thing the author did at first, why the human being put itself in this situations, knowing it would suffer a lot? Nowadays, what I think is that death is part of life. We are determinated to meet it, we will met a lot, and even do we live our life not thinking about it all the time (would be really hard otherwise) it is inevitable and it is part of the big circle that make life what it is. Is worth it, suffer that pain. Sounds crazy but, the good experiences, the joy, the warm moments, are all worth it.
The author makes then a comparison between this concept and the concept of a summer romance, both are idealistically the same, intense and with a due date. In the case of this book, it had a happy ending but, you know, summer romance are not really meant to last..

With that said, Things I like it ..


## It was good

- I like reading books where the protagonist is a mom. I think is a challenge to write love sotry with kids in it. I really think that kids could be a really good resource in romace books if it's well written and Anna (out author) does really well. I really liked the two girls in this book.

- Well I was checking my notes and aparently Ethan is a good flirting guy, I don't remember anymore his flirting skills but sure it must been good. A good male lead is always key factor in making a book good (and I am not talking about the flirting haha). I like that he was a very straightforward type of guy, and even do his family made it dificult for him, he found his way out, he grew up and became a person who chose to help others (skateboarding park, yes), I didn't like his final decision but we will go with that in a minute..

- The grow of Anna was convincent. I have a soft spot for this. I will rate a book higher if somehow I feel like the story is convincent and that the protagonist feels real and they simply move me. I didn't necessary feel all this things with Anna, but I respect her and I feel.. how do I say it? satisfy with her growth. I am really glad she recognize in the end that she was sad, and that she had to step up to find herself again. I will always cheer this type of stories.

-I like how Anna in the end comes back to work. It was something that she lost when she had her children and I thought she wouldn't get it back, but she does. That was satisfying.


## It was ... something

- Some parts of Anna.. I really didn't like the thing at the end where the message felt like 'I have to be a strong mother for my children'. Don't get me wrong, I don't think that was the message or it was but not in the way I am making it feel haha, at some parts it felt like that was..like the key for Anna, especially when she had this chat with her daughter and she said a lot of things, that I don't know how Anna took that, I think this is a thing that I will only understand if I were a mother. That's why even do I am not agree? I kind of get it.. I think haha

-His ex.. was a thing..But, for the sake of the plot, he has to be like that and I read other book where the ex is even worst.. I would get on that book eventualy hehe. Anyway, I dislike him but it was good for the plot so it really shouldn't be in this category, uh? But, one thing. I think I missed something about this ex. I didn't like him but I missed something. I don't know what. I like the part where Anna finally stand up against him, that was good.

-I really, reeeally didn't like it Ethan's decision.. Him coming back to the old town? Him giving up his life because I quote 'I realized you are where I belong' Uh oh.. It's a nop for me. I understand and it is the 'cliche' finale.. we can say that, but Idk, I really enjoy the entire book when you didn't really know if they were going to stay together because of the thing that both live far and it was a lot. Ali decision to cut it off because she thought she couldn't bare the pain of make him live with her it was really good, but at the end he was willing to give up his life for her. I am really not a fan of that but I know that this is the things you will find in.. romance hehe , happy endings. But in a bright side, he did realize the place where he lives doesn't necesarilly defines who he was. He was what make the places where he is so shiny and people falling for him and creating a community, you know? That message is good but still I not fond of his being 'the man who solves'.. but we can pin this idea and discussed in another post, another day haha


## Final beredict

A solid 3/5 for this book. It was an entertaining read, I'm not gonna lie, I went from 20% to 70% in a day (the percentage of pages read). It was a cozy read ideallity to appreciated the path of the characters and just to vibe with them, and be mad when they suffer, you know? haha
