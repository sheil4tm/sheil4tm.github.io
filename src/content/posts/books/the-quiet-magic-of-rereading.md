Some books wait patiently on the shelf until the right season finds you again.

I picked up a novel I last finished years ago, expecting nostalgia and little else. Instead I found new sentences underlined by a quieter version of myself — passages that barely registered the first time. Re-reading isn’t about “getting more” from a story so much as noticing who you’ve become between visits.

## What changed

The plot was still the plot. The characters still made the same choices. What shifted was the weather around them: patience where I once wanted speed, tenderness where I once wanted cleverness.

If you’re between new reads and feel restless, try a re-read. Let the shelf surprise you with something you already own.
