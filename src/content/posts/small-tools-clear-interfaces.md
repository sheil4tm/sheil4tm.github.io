I’ve been trimming a side project down to the tools that actually reduce friction.

Fancy abstractions feel productive until you’re debugging them at midnight. Clear folders, obvious names, and a build that finishes before you lose the thought — that’s the real luxury.

## A tiny checklist

1. Can a future-you find the entry point in under a minute?
2. Does the local preview feel instant enough to stay curious?
3. Are errors readable without opening three docs tabs?

None of this is glamorous. All of it compounds. Tech shelves don’t only hold frameworks — they hold habits.
